Our story

Named after a promise, not a marketing plan.

Beth White runs this place with her son, out of an old log cabin at a crossroads outside North Lewisburg. There's no sign on the highway telling you it's here — most people find it because someone else told them to.

The name comes from her niece, who couldn't quite say "Aunt Beth" when she was first learning to talk, so it came out "Uncle Beth" instead — and it stuck. Beth opened the restaurant in her niece's memory after she passed away in a car accident, and the name never changed.

Everything on the menu is made from scratch, the way Beth puts it: "restaurants make food out of boxes and bags, we make everything from scratch." That's the whole philosophy, and it's why snowmobilers, tractor drivers, Jaguar owners and at least one customer on horseback have all ended up parked outside the same log cabin.
