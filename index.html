The name

Not a marketing plan.

Beth White runs this place with her son, out of an old log cabin at a rural crossroads. There's no sign from the highway — most people find it because someone else told them to.

The name comes from her niece, who couldn't quite say "Aunt Beth" as a toddler, so it came out "Uncle Beth" — and it stuck. Beth opened the restaurant in her memory after she passed away in a car accident. Everything's made from scratch: "restaurants make food out of boxes and bags, we make everything from scratch."

It's probably some of the best BBQ I've ever had. — TripAdvisor review
