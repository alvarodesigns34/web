The beginning

A house by the sea.

Opened 23 January 2006 at Navigation Point, looking over Hartlepool Marina. The name is a nod to Café del Mar.

Spanish at heart.By the water.

The kitchen mixes Spanish tapas with Mexican and North African plates: mussels, gambas, chorizo in red wine, chimichurri steak, fajitas and paella. Walk-ins are welcome.

A taste of Casa

Made to share.

01 · Seafood

Mejillones

Fresh steamed mussels in a spicy tomato sauce, or in white wine, garlic and cream.

02 · The pan

Paella de mariscos

Spanish rice with seafood and vegetables, served in a small pan.

03 · From the oil

Gambas pil pil

King prawns in garlic and chilli oil.

04 · Large plates

Solomillo con pimienta

Sirloin steak with peppercorn sauce — a regular on the large-plate list.

05 · The skillet

Fajitas

Hot skillet with peppers and onions, tortillas, salsa and sour cream. Vegetarian, chicken, steak or king prawn.

Most tables eat from the Rapido: three tapas, or one large plate plus one tapa.

Sangria on the sign. Tables that stay.

Most tables stay one to two hours. Walk-ins are welcome; any size can be booked on the phone.
