The house by the sea

Spanish spirit, right on the marina.

Casa Del Mar opened on 23 January 2006 at Navigation Point, looking over Hartlepool Marina. The name is a nod to Café del Mar — the house by the sea.

The kitchen mixes Spanish tapas with Mexican and North African plates: mussels, gambas, chorizo in red wine, chimichurri steak, fajitas and paella. Walk-ins are welcome; tables of any size can be booked on the phone.

The experience

Come for the water. Stay for the table.

01

Tapas

Small plates for the middle of the table. Olives, halloumi, bravas, fried brie — eaten slowly, with company.

02

Paella & plates

Seafood paella in a small pan, mussels, gambas, and large plates of steak and salmon.

03

Drinks & good times

Sangria on the sign, a marina at the door, and tables that tend to stay one to two hours.

From the kitchen

Made for sharing.

Most tables eat from the Rapido: three tapas, or one large plate plus one tapa. The full list sits behind each category — not on the landing.

House favourites

What the table orders twice.

Mejillones

Fresh steamed mussels in a spicy tomato sauce, or in white wine, garlic and cream.

Paella de mariscos

Spanish rice with seafood and vegetables, served in a small pan.

Gambas pil pil

King prawns in garlic and chilli oil.

Solomillo con pimienta

Sirloin steak with peppercorn sauce — a regular on the large-plate list.
