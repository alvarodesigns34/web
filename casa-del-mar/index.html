House by the sea

Casa Del Mar opened on 23 January 2006 at Navigation Point, looking over Hartlepool Marina. The name is a nod to Café del Mar — the house by the sea.

The kitchen mixes Spanish tapas with Mexican and North African plates: mussels, gambas, chorizo in red wine, chimichurri steak, fajitas and paella. Walk-ins are welcome; tables of any size can be booked on the phone.
